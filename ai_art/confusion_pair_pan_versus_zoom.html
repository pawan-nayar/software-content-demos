Pan vs. Zoom

Field handbook for Midjourney Pan vs. Photoshop Generative Expand

Understanding the difference between expanding your canvas and changing scale is essential for AI artists. This guide clarifies the common confusion between Pan (in Midjourney) and Zoom (in Photoshop Generative Fill) so teams stop miniaturizing desktop layouts on phones.

1. What Each Tool Really Does

↔️

Pan (Midjourney)

Extends the canvas in exactly one direction per command (up | down | left | right). The existing image stays full size and simply slides toward the opposite edge while new content is generated in the requested direction.

🔍

Zoom Out (Photoshop)

Shrinks the original image proportionally and surrounds it with newly generated context on all four sides. Think of stepping back with a drone to reveal worldbuilding everywhere at once.

Common misconception: assuming Pan behaves like Zoom Out or that Zoom Out can target a single edge. The result is desktop-sized cards shrunk onto phones instead of reflowed layouts.

3. Memory Aids

🧭

Pan = Panorama

Arrow Thinking
Slides sideways like moving a camera track. Adds world only where the arrow points. Great for tightening compositions without shrinking originals.
🎯

Zoom = Circle

Omnidirectional
Shrinks the subject to reveal perimeter context. Works like stepping backward with a drone. One command, all edges updated.
🚁

Camera Slide vs. Drone Rise

Motion Analogy
Pan = dolly move (horizontal). Zoom Out = drone up + back. Different moves, different intent.
