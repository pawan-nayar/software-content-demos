📋 How This Quiz Works

Welcome to the GitHub Mastery Rubric Quiz Series! This isn't a typical quiz—it's designed to build your judgment through real-world scenarios.

What makes this different:

  • Multi-criteria rubric: Each question evaluates 4 dimensions (Accuracy, Process, Clarity, Risk Awareness)
  • Weighted scoring: Criteria have different weights (10-40%) reflecting their importance
  • Partial credit: You earn points for partially correct answers, not just all-or-nothing
  • Detailed feedback: After submitting, you'll see why each answer scored the way it did

How to proceed:

  • Read the scenario carefully
  • Select one answer per criterion
  • Click Submit to see your score and feedback
  • Use Prev/Next to navigate between quizzes
  • Complete all 10 quizzes to see your final summary
